    farmer crosses alone to the near bank
    farmer crosses with wolf to the far bank
    ...stuck: every remaining legal move either revisits a state or carries an item back -- and its one rule forbids exactly the move the solution needs.

Tree search (BFS): solved=True nodes_expanded=10 solution_length=7
    1. farmer crosses with goat to the far bank
    2. farmer crosses alone to the near bank
    3. farmer crosses with wolf to the far bank
    4. farmer crosses with goat to the near bank
    5. farmer crosses with cabbage to the far bank
    6. farmer crosses alone to the near bank
    7. farmer crosses with goat to the far bank

The solution contains 1 'backwards' item-carrying move(s) -- the goat must be ferried back once. A linear chain that refuses to retreat can never find it; search can, because retreating is just another branch.
